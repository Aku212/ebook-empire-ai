# Ebook Empire AI

Ebook Empire AI is a free Claude AI Agent that helps creators research profitable ebook opportunities and build a complete publishing workflow.

It combines market research, writing, product creation, and marketing into one structured process.

## Features

✅ Discover ebook niches

✅ Validate demand

✅ Analyze competition

✅ Create complete outlines

✅ Write publication-ready chapters

✅ Generate landing pages

✅ Produce launch content

✅ Create social media campaigns

✅ Build email sequences

✅ Generate a 30-day marketing plan

## Perfect For

- First-time authors
- Digital product creators
- Freelancers
- Coaches
- Bloggers
- Marketers
- Entrepreneurs

## How to Use

1. Copy PROMPT.md
2. Open Claude
3. Paste the prompt
4. Describe your topic or audience.
5. Follow the generated research, writing, and launch workflow.

## Disclaimer

This agent is a research and writing assistant.

Success depends on the quality of your work, marketing, pricing, audience, and many other factors. It does not guarantee sales or income.
